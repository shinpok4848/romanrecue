# SunoFlow generation contract

Treat these as permanent repository rules for future generation changes.

- Fold new real-world Suno prompt examples into stable preset data in `assets/js/genrePresets.js`; do not bolt one-off prose onto the generator.
- Preserve one-way semantic coherence: user theme → lane-aware curated concepts (narrative Wednesday vs high-motion Friday when a compound theme supports both) → bilingual title pair → hook containing `titleKo` exactly → complete Korean lyrics → compatible Mood and sonic concept. A linked Short must derive from that parent and copy its exact strongest-hook lines.
- Preserve the three dedicated one-click modes and stable palette IDs: **위로곡** → `comfort-embrace` (quiet permission to rest or cry, never pressure to overcome), **응원곡** → `encouragement-forward` (starting line, falling and rising, friends and forward stadium energy), and **행복한 음악** → `everyday-happiness` (sunlight, bread, handclaps, friends, and joy already present). Do not merge their authored line banks or make their narratives interchangeable.
- Every dedicated mode must produce the normal 4-week v2 package: eight complete Korean Full songs using its requested palette plus eight exact parent-derived Shorts. Guidance-only lyrics do not satisfy a mode. Keep all exact Korean/English aliases owned by the dedicated palette, preserve three-way compound routing (`emotional` comfort, `energetic` encouragement, `balanced` happiness), and keep these explicit modes out of unknown-theme fallbacks.
- Quick buttons must write their fixed explicit `data-theme` to the existing root `theme` and call the same generation path as custom submit, with a fresh seed on every click including repeats. Root `theme` remains the sole persisted mode source: restore selection only for an exact v2 theme match, never infer it for legacy plans, and do not add a UI-mode schema field.
- Keep the exact 15 canonical structures, IDs, section labels, and section order in `assets/js/structureProfiles.js`. Apply their producer rules in generated content, including silent instrumental/solo/interlude/key-change/end tags, one directly placed key change at most, Build up resolution, story-changing Verse 2, transition bridge, and explicit End/Fade Out.
- Future presets, palette adaptations, and structure changes must preserve truthful section cue generation from the effective preset's real instruments, selected vocal/BPM/Key, and palette semantics. Keep each cue focused on one concern, mention at most two actual instruments, and emit complete prose within the 90-character descriptor, 135-character cue, and 105-character Short-source limits without truncation. Keep cue metadata out of `lyricSections[].lines` so Korean lyrics and exact Shorts/YouTube excerpts stay uncontaminated.
- Preserve plan-wide direction integrity: a schema-v2 month is either wholly cue-less or enriched across all 16 tracks. For enriched sheets, validate every renderer-emitted singer/performance tag against gender and the structure's exact optional vocal contrast, and keep each Short `lyricsGuide` equal to its canonical parent-hook prefix plus rendered lyrics.
- Keep Suno v6 `Mood` as a dedicated field distinct from `Style Prompt`. Continue the proven Style spine and retain technical/genre negatives in `Exclude Prompt`.
- SunoFlow prepares text packages for songs generated by the user in their own Suno workflow. Do not add a Suno API, server-side generation, uploads, credentials, telemetry, or claims that SunoFlow generated the audio.
- Preserve the dependency-free GitHub Pages architecture: vanilla HTML, hand-written CSS, browser ES modules, no framework, package install, CDN, build step, or runtime external request.
- Keep explicit seeded generation deterministic through isolated named streams. Browser Generate actions should continue to pass a fresh safe seed.
- Keep schema-v1 plans honest and readable. Never fabricate v2 Korean lyrics for legacy English guidance, and never rename the `sunoflow.plan.v1` storage key without an explicit migration decision.
